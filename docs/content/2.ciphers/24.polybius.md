---
title: Polybius square
description: Each letter becomes a row digit and a column digit in a 5×5 grid, I and J sharing a cell. An optional keyword reorders the grid.
---

::cipher-facts{name="polybius"}
::

The alphabet fills a 5×5 grid, J folded into I. A letter is its row and column, both 1 to 5, pairs separated by spaces. With a `key` the keyword goes first, duplicates removed, and the rest of the alphabet follows - the same square Playfair and Bifid build.

```ts
const polybius = create("polybius");
polybius.encode("HELLO").text; // "23 15 31 31 34"
polybius.encode("JULIUS").text; // "24 45 31 24 45 43", J is 24 like I
polybius.encode("HELLO", { key: "KEYWORD" }).text; // "33 12 35 35 15"
polybius.decode("23 15 31 31 34").text; // "HELLO"
```

Decoding reads digit pairs and leaves anything else in place, so `"23 ab 24"` becomes `"HabI"`. [Tap code](/ciphers/tap-code) is the same grid with C and K sharing instead of I and J. [Bifid](/ciphers/bifid) and [ADFGVX](/ciphers/adfgvx) build on the coordinates.
